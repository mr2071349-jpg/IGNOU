INSTRUCTIONS:

  1. Read  the details of programme including eligibility criteria, fee details, duration, etc.  instructions carefully:
    1. Click on the button NEW REGISTRATION that appears in the applicant login area and fill the required registration details.
    2. Remember, while choosing your UserName it must be between 8 to 16 characters.
    3. While choosing your password it must be alphanumeric and between 8 to 16 characters long.
    4. After filling the mandatory information click the "SUBMIT" button.
    5. Your username will be instantly sent to you via e-mail and SMS.
  2. Remember your Username and Password for subsequent login.
  3. If you have already registered i.e you are an existing user click the "LOGIN" button.
  4. Before proceeding for filling the form online the applicant must have the following:-
    1. Clear, Color, Passport size photograph with white background (less than 100 KB in JPEG format)
    2. Softcopy of Signature (less than 100 KB in JPEG format)
    3. Softcopy of relevant Educational Qualification (less than 200 KB in JPEG / PDF format)
    4. Softcopy of Experience Certificate (if any) (less than 200 KB in JPEG / PDF format)
    5. Softcopy of Category Certificate, if SC/ST/OBC (less than 200 KB in JPEG / PDF format)
  5. Fee can be paid by the following methods:
    • Credit Card (Master/Visa)
    • Debit Card (Master/Visa/Rupay)
    • Net Banking
    • UPI
  6. It is necessary to scan documents from your originals. Once you have uploaded the document, click the next button you will get the Form preview option. Save/Print your form for future reference.

Registration fee, Cancellation of Admission and Refund of Fee

  1. The Registration Fee is non-refundable.
  2. The fee paid shall be refunded prior to confirmation of admission.
  3. An amount equivalent to 15% of the programme fee, subject to a ceiling of Rs.2,000/-, will be deducted from the fee paid in case the request for cancellation is received after the confirmation of admission.
  4. In case a student has opted for soft copy of the SLM, the fee paid will be refunded after deducting the Registration fee only.
  5. If a request for cancellation is received from a student who has availed fee exemption and has paid only the Registration fee and Development fee, only the Development fee will be refunded.
  6. No Refund of fee will be admissible after 60 days of the closing date of the admissions.

For more details, please refer to the Common Prospectus.
